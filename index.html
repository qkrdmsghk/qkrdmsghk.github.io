About

I am a postdoctoral fellow at the Innocore AI-CRED Institute, KAIST, advised by Prof. Sungsoo Ahn and Prof. Woo Youn Kim. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Seoul National University under Prof. Sun Kim, where my dissertation on subgraph-informed hierarchical learning was recognized with the Outstanding Doctoral Dissertation Award. My research develops graph-based and multi-modal AI for drug discovery and biomedical reasoning, integrating molecular, cellular, sequential, textual, and knowledge-graph signals across the preclinical pipeline, with a growing focus on LLM-based scientific reasoning over biological knowledge toward trustworthy, biology-grounded systems for drug discovery and personalized medicine.

Robust & generalizable graph learning

A model is only useful if it holds up on data it was never trained on. I design graph methods that stay reliable under distribution shift and transfer across domains.
